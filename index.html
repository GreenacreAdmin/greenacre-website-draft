Welcome to Greenacre

Welcome to Greenacre International School

Greenacre is an inclusive international school in the natural surroundings of Koh Samui, providing a clear educational journey from age 3 through to Cambridge IGCSE at age 16. Academic learning is enriched by co-curricular opportunities, cultural understanding and care for the natural world.

Cambridge pathway

A clear route to Cambridge IGCSE

Cambridge IGCSE is an internationally recognised qualification typically studied by students aged 14 to 16. At Greenacre, it forms the upper stage of a progressive learning pathway, preparing students for further study including A Levels and other post-16 routes.
