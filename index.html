Welcome to Greenacre

Welcome to Greenacre International School

Greenacre is an inclusive international school based in the natural surroundings of Koh Samui, providing a clear educational journey from age 3 through to Cambridge IGCSE.

We support students to be the best version of themselves through academic learning, co-curricular opportunities, cultural understanding and a love for the planet.
