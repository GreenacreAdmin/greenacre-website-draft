Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to some of the questions families commonly ask when considering Greenacre.

Greenacre at a glance

Questions from families

At what age can my child start?

Greenacre's learning journey begins in Early Years from age 3. Learn more about Early Years →

How does the admissions process work?

Our Admissions & Visits page explains the application process and how to arrange a visit to Greenacre.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes. Families are encouraged to visit with their child during a normal school day, meet the team and experience the atmosphere of Greenacre for themselves. Learn about admissions and visits →

What documents do I need to apply?

Families will usually need to provide identification documents for the student and parents or guardians, together with relevant previous school records where applicable. Requirements may vary depending on the student's circumstances, so please contact Admissions for confirmation of the documents required for your application. Contact Admissions →

How do I get the current fees?

The 2026/27 fee schedule is available on the Fees page. For questions about fees or the application process, contact Admissions. View current fees →

What are the school day times?

For school hours, arrival and collection information, see our Key Information page.

Does Greenacre provide school transport?

Information about school transport and current routes is available on our Services page.

Are meals provided at school?

Information about school meals and catering is available on our Services page.

Do students wear uniform?

Yes. Greenacre has school uniform requirements. Current practical information and uniform details are available in Parent Information. View uniform information →

What curriculum does Greenacre follow?

Greenacre follows the Cambridge International Programme alongside aspects of the UK Curriculum. The learning journey runs from Early Years through Primary and Secondary to Cambridge IGCSE. Greenacre is a Cambridge Approved Examinations Centre. Explore our curriculum →

Does Greenacre support students learning English?

Yes. The school's approach includes support for EAL learners and teaching is adapted so that students can participate and make progress. Explore our curriculum →

What qualifications do students work towards in Secondary?

Students progress towards Cambridge IGCSE in Years 10 and 11. The current pathway includes English Language, English as a Second Language, Mathematics, Co-ordinated Sciences, Geography, Global Perspectives and Art & Design. Learn more about Secondary →

Will my child have homework?

In Primary, homework is generally given once a week, although teachers may assign targeted practice when it is beneficial. In Secondary, homework varies across subjects but is always carefully focused on engaging students and helping them consolidate their learning and make progress.

How can I help my child at home?

Reading regularly with your child at home is one of the most valuable ways to support their learning. Even a few minutes of shared reading each day can help build confidence, fluency and enjoyment of reading.

Are extra-curricular activities available?

Yes. Clubs and extra-curricular activities are offered as part of wider school life. The current programme is published separately because activities can change from term to term. View clubs and ECAs →
