Going home

School bus - front gate

Bus students wait inside the front gate. Any change of plan or bus query goes to Krung first - your child's tutor can also help.

Collected by a parent - back gate

Students wait inside the back gate until their parent arrives - everyone is collected, and no student leaves without a teacher knowing. If plans change, please let the tutor know in the morning.

What students need every day

Everyone

Blue or black pen, red pen, pencil, sharpener, ruler, scientific calculator, scissors, glue stick, water bottle and a sun hat. On PE days, students come to school in Greenacre PE kit with proper PE shoes - no PE shoes means no PE.

Years 7 - 9

English, Maths and Science textbooks on the days they are needed - homework comes from them.

Years 10 & 11

A laptop or tablet, charged and ready to use, every day - plus their textbooks on the days they are needed, as homework comes from them.

Uniform & appearance

Students come to school in full Greenacre uniform every day, and their appearance must also meet the school's requirements - closed flat shoes, long hair fully tied back, stud earrings only and no excessive jewellery, short nails, and no unnatural hair colours. The full uniform and appearance requirements are on the school website.

Day-to-day information

The essentials families ask about most.

Attendance & absence

The school year is 187 days and our minimum expectation is 95% attendance. If your child is absent or late, please email reception at admin@greenacre.ac.th - you can copy in the tutor. Medical or immigration documents also go to reception.

Mobile phones

Phones are not used in school at all. A phone brought in for the bus journey is handed in at reception on arrival and returned at the end of the day. Any other phone must be agreed in advance with Teacher Dara and handed to the office or to Teacher Ellis, to be locked away in the Science room for the day.

Homework

English is set on Tuesdays, Science on Wednesdays (Thursdays for Year 9) and Maths on Fridays; other subjects set work on other days. Every task is posted on Google Classroom - please encourage your child to check it each evening.

Lunch & snacks

School lunch runs on a two-week rota with a vegetarian option, salad bar and hummus every day. Packed lunches go into the secondary lunch box on the green first thing - they are refrigerated for the day. Snacks must be healthy: no sweets, candy or chocolate.

After-school clubs (ECAs) - Years 7 - 9

Clubs run Monday to Friday, 15:15 - 16:00, including sports, arts and the Duke of Edinburgh Survival Club. They are for Years 7 - 9 only, as Years 10 and 11 have a timetabled lesson in Period 7. Sign up through the school office - 250 THB per club, per term. Places are limited, so register early.

Planning a trip? Please check the assessment and exam dates first

Attendance during assessment and exam weeks matters more than at any other time of year. Please avoid booking travel during the assessment weeks listed below. For Year 11, the Cambridge IGCSE examinations (26 April - 9 June 2027) are externally fixed and cannot be rearranged under any circumstances.
