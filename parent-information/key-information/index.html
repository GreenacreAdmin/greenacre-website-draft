Key Information

Practical information for Greenacre families, including the school day, uniform, meals, transport and communication.

Everyday information

What families need to know

Current parents: for day-to-day school enquiries, email the school office at contact@greenacre.ac.th.

Uniform

Students from Early Years through Secondary wear Greenacre school uniform. Separate PE and swimming kit is also used. Please contact the school directly for further uniform details.

Lunch

Families can choose the school lunch or send a packed lunch. Vegetarian and vegan options are available.

Transport

School transport is arranged in three zones. See Services for the zone boundaries, and contact the school to confirm routes and pick-up arrangements.

Homework

Primary homework is generally set weekly, with targeted practice where useful. Secondary homework varies by subject and is focused on consolidating learning and progress.

Parent communication

Primary families receive class updates through ClassDojo. Secondary uses Google Classroom for learning updates and communication with students and parents.

Reading at home

Greenacre encourages families to read regularly with children at home as part of developing confidence, fluency and enjoyment of reading.
