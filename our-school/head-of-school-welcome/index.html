Head of School Welcome

Welcome to Greenacre International School. At Greenacre, we are committed to providing every member of our community with opportunities to grow and excel as we follow the School’s motto:

Growing together, learning together, working together.

On behalf of the whole Greenacre International School community, I am delighted to welcome you to our school.

Greenacre is a warm, inclusive and internationally minded community where students, families and staff come together from many different backgrounds and cultures. We believe that education is about much more than academic achievement. It is about developing confident, compassionate and curious young people who are prepared to make a positive difference in the world around them.

Our dedicated teachers, teaching assistants, administration and support staff work together to create a positive and purposeful learning environment in which every child is known, valued and encouraged to succeed.

We want every member of our community to have the opportunity to grow together, learn together and work together. Our aim is simple: to help every student become the best version of themselves.

We encourage every member of the Greenacre community to contribute their ideas, share their strengths, embrace new opportunities and play an active role in the continued growth of our school. We want Greenacre to be a place where students, families and staff feel valued, supported and proud to belong.

I look forward to working together as we continue to build an inspiring school community and create the very best opportunities for our students.

Welcome to Greenacre International School.

Warm regards,
Mr Dara Nagle
Head of School
