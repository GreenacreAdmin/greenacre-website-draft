Greenacre International School / Koh Samui, Thailand

Wellbeing

A safe, inclusive and supportive community is central to helping every Greenacre student learn, grow and fulfil their potential.

Known and valued

Wellbeing is part of learning

Greenacre aims to create a positive and purposeful environment in which every child is known, valued and encouraged to succeed. Academic development and personal development are not treated as separate goals. Staff wellbeing is also valued, helping to create a positive, connected and sustainable working environment across the school.

Our GREEN values of Respect, Empathy and Nurture help create a sense of nurture and wellbeing throughout the school, with kindness, belonging, resilience and mutual respect embedded in everyday school life.

Our approach

Relationships, responsibility and belonging

Restorative practice

Restorative practice helps students understand the impact of their actions, take responsibility and work towards repairing relationships when difficulties arise. The approach supports accountability, respect and constructive problem-solving rather than focusing only on punishment.

Inclusive support

Teaching is adapted to recognise different starting points and learning needs, including those of students with SEND and EAL.

Personal growth

Students are encouraged to develop confidence, resilience, gratitude, mindfulness, compassion and self-respect, with kindness and giving back encouraged as part of school life.

International community

Different backgrounds, one school community

Greenacre brings together students, families and staff from many backgrounds and cultures. Cultural awareness and respectful communication are part of everyday school life.

Our aim is for every student to feel supported and encouraged to become a confident, compassionate and curious young person who can make a positive difference.
