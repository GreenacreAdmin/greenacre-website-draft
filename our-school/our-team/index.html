Our Team

Meet the teachers and support staff who make up the Greenacre community.

Our Amazing Team

We are exceptionally proud of our team here at Greenacre International School.

Our teachers and support staff are committed and dedicated to ensuring that our students have the best school experience possible. Whether that is through the exceptional and inspiring teaching or the way in which the whole team is valued as an integral part of the Greenacre family.

All our teachers are highly-qualified and our teaching standards ensure rapid progress for students of all abilities. Our competent classroom assistants all have previous experience within an international school environment.
