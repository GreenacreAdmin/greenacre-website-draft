Our Team

Meet the teachers and support staff who make up the Greenacre community.

Our Dedicated Team

Our team brings together qualified teachers, teaching assistants and support staff from a range of backgrounds.

All Greenacre teachers are appropriately qualified for their roles and work closely with students to provide engaging, well-pitched learning and support individual progress. Our teaching assistants provide valuable classroom and pastoral support across the school.

Together, our staff contribute to the welcoming, supportive and internationally minded community at Greenacre.
