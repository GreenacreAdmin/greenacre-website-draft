About Greenacre

An international school with a global outlook, based on Koh Samui and shaped by a warm, inclusive community.

Our school identity

Growing Together · Learning Together · Working Together

Greenacre is an international school providing a progressive learning pathway from age 3 to 16. The curriculum combines internationally recognised programmes with elements of the UK curriculum, progressing to Cambridge IGCSE in Years 10 and 11.

Our setting in Thailand is an important part of our identity. We celebrate the diverse backgrounds and experiences within our community and strongly value the traditions and culture of Thailand, helping students become open-minded, respectful and confident global citizens.

Our GREEN values are woven throughout school life. Sustainability, environmental awareness and care for our community are not simply classroom topics, but part of how we learn and live together.

Our GREEN values

Values lived throughout school life

GGrowthContinuous learning, personal development and resilience, with students encouraged to set goals, improve and reflect.
RRespectMutual respect across the school community, celebrating diversity and encouraging thoughtful communication and collaboration.
EEnvironmentalProtecting the planet, supporting practical green initiatives and encouraging students to become environmental stewards.
EEmpathyCompassion and understanding, with active listening, kindness and a community in which people feel valued and heard.

Mission

Every person matters

Greenacre aims to provide a stimulating and enriching education within a supportive, inclusive community, where each person matters and in which all students have the opportunity to thrive and fulfil their potential as a global citizen.

Our aims

Preparing students for life beyond school

We want every Greenacre student to be equipped for life beyond school, confident, responsible and ready to contribute.

Confident learners

Develop confidence and high self-respect, supporting students in achieving their ambitions.

Responsible citizens

Become environmentally aware global citizens who make a positive contribution to their communities.

High expectations

Experience a culture of excellence that stretches and challenges all.

Supported to thrive

Learn in a safe environment, with parents and carers working in partnership to support learning and personal development.

Learning at Greenacre

A broad, connected curriculum

Students progress from Early Years through Primary and Secondary to Cambridge IGCSE, developing strong subject knowledge alongside confidence, creativity, critical thinking and international mindedness.

Education for the whole child

Our approach combines academic progress with inclusion, cultural understanding, environmental responsibility and personal development. We aim to help every student become the best version of themselves.

Safeguarding

A safe and supportive school

Greenacre is committed to safeguarding and promoting the wellbeing of every student. The school maintains comprehensive safeguarding policies and procedures, supported by regular staff training, so that safeguarding responsibilities are understood across the school community. Our aim is to provide a safe and inclusive environment where students feel valued, heard and supported.

Greenacre on Koh Samui

A licensed international school in southern Koh Samui

Greenacre International School is a privately owned international school licensed to operate in Thailand. The school is based at its registered address at 108 Moo 2, Na Mueang, Koh Samui, Surat Thani 84140.
