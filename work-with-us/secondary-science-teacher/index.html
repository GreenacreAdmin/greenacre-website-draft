Secondary Science Teacher

Koh Samui, Thailand. Starting January 2027.

Join our growing international school

Greenacre International School is seeking an enthusiastic, dedicated and suitably qualified Secondary Science Teacher to join our growing international school on Koh Samui, Thailand.

We currently teach students from Years 7-11, following the Cambridge International curriculum, and are continuing to develop our Secondary provision.

With a new purpose-equipped Science laboratory opening, this is an exciting opportunity for the successful candidate to help develop practical Science teaching, inspire young scientists and contribute to the continued growth of our school.

The role

The successful candidate will:

  • Teach Science across Years 7-11.
  • Deliver engaging lessons across Biology, Chemistry and Physics.
  • Teach the Cambridge Lower Secondary and IGCSE Science curriculum.
  • Plan and deliver practical experiments and scientific investigations.
  • Contribute to the development and effective use of our new Science laboratory.
  • Monitor student progress, assess learning and provide constructive feedback.
  • Support students with different learning needs and levels of English proficiency.
  • Contribute to curriculum planning and the wider life of the school.

The ideal candidate

We are looking for someone who:

  • Holds a recognised teaching qualification (PGCE, BEd or equivalent).
  • Has a degree in Science or a relevant scientific subject.
  • Has previous experience teaching secondary Science.
  • Is confident delivering practical Science lessons and maintaining laboratory safety.
  • Has experience with Cambridge International or IGCSE curricula (desirable).
  • Has high expectations of student achievement, behaviour and engagement.
  • Is collaborative, flexible and enthusiastic about contributing to a developing international school.

Salary and benefits

  • Monthly salary: ฿65,000, depending on qualifications and experience.
  • Housing allowance.
  • Health insurance.
  • Visa and work permit assistance.
  • Paid holidays and annual leave in accordance with the employment contract and school calendar.
  • Professional development and ongoing training opportunities.
  • School fee concessions for eligible dependent children, subject to school policy.
  • A welcoming, supportive and collaborative international teaching environment.

About Greenacre

Greenacre International School provides a supportive and inclusive learning environment where students are encouraged to develop academically, socially and personally. Our values are reflected in our commitment to Growing Together, Learning Together, Working Together.

We are looking for a teacher who shares these values and wants to play an active role in the continued development of our school.
