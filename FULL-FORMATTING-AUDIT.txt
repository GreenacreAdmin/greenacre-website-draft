White-space/layout review
-------------------------
The audit searched for empty structural elements, float-based positioning, unusually large fixed
content heights, oversized explicit margins/padding and grid/flex structures likely to reserve
unused space. No unexplained fixed-height or empty-layout spacing problems were found.

The large fixed blocks that remain on the homepage belong to the map and Facebook feed and are
intentional. Hero spacing is also intentional and was excluded from the white-space warning check.

Changes made
------------
1. Consolidated the light-background eyebrow/kicker contrast rule into one shared stylesheet:
   /assets/formatting-safeguards.css
2. Added safe max-width rules for images, video, iframes and tables.
3. Added min-width:0 to grid/flex children to prevent accidental horizontal misalignment caused
   by long text or embedded content.
4. Removed the obsolete Stage 12 About-page scroll-spacing experiment.
5. No content wording, roster data, links or deliberate page layouts were changed.
