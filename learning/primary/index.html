Primary

Progressive, inclusive learning through Years 1-6, with an increasing Cambridge Primary focus across the curriculum.

Years 1-6

Building strong foundations for future learning

Primary develops knowledge, skills, confidence and independence across a broad curriculum. Greenacre's current approach combines the Cambridge International Programme with aspects of the UK Curriculum.

Teaching supports academic progression while recognising the different needs of learners. Students are encouraged to think, communicate, collaborate and reflect on their learning.

Primary learning

Developing the whole child

Greenacre is passionate about helping every child get the most from their education and develop as a lifelong learner. Primary combines internationally recognised learning with aspects of the UK Curriculum, adapted to Greenacre's setting in Thailand and its environmental vision.

Strong foundationsEnglish, Mathematics and Science develop secure knowledge and skills with clear progression through Years 1-6.
Broad learningStudents also learn across humanities, creative subjects, computing, physical education and the wider life of the school.
Personal developmentConfidence, self-awareness, respect and responsibility are developed alongside academic learning.
ProgressTeachers use ongoing assessment to understand learning, respond to individual needs and plan appropriate next steps.

Learning beyond a score

Challenge, support and reflection

Assessment is used to improve learning, not simply to measure it. Evidence from lessons and recognised Cambridge assessment approaches helps teachers understand progress, plan next steps and provide appropriate support and challenge.

Across Primary, the wider aim is to develop confident learners who approach new ideas with curiosity, resilience and growing independence. Practical activities, creative work, environmental learning and opportunities beyond the classroom help children discover interests, enjoy learning and take an active part in school life.
