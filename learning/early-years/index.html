Early Years

A happy, secure and creative beginning where children learn through play, investigation and doing.

The start of the journey

Curiosity comes first

Children can join Greenacre's Early Years from age 3, beginning a learning journey that continues through Primary and Secondary to Cambridge IGCSE. They are supported to explore, communicate, build confidence and develop positive relationships in an inclusive environment.

Learning at this stage is active and purposeful, with opportunities to investigate, create, play and learn from the world around them.

Growing at Greenacre
How children learn at Greenacre

More than preparation for Primary

Early Years is a distinct and important stage of learning. Children develop academically, socially and personally while becoming familiar with Greenacre's GREEN values and the wider school community.

As children progress, the aim is a confident transition into Primary while preserving curiosity, creativity and a positive attitude to learning.

Early Years learning framework

A broad foundation for development

Early Years learning is shaped around seven connected areas, supporting children's communication, physical, personal, social, creative and academic development.

Communication & Language

Listening, understanding and expressing ideas through conversation and interaction.

Physical Development

Movement, coordination, confidence and the foundations of healthy, active development.

Personal, Social & Emotional Development

Relationships, independence, self-awareness and learning to understand feelings and others.

Literacy

Developing enjoyment of stories, language, early reading and writing.

Mathematics

Exploring number, pattern, shape, space and mathematical ideas through meaningful experiences.

Understanding the World

Making sense of people, communities, nature, places and the world around us.

Expressive Arts & Design

Exploring imagination and creativity through art, music, movement, materials and play.
