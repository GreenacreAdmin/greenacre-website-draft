Greenacre International School / Koh Samui, Thailand

Secondary

Cambridge International learning in Years 7-9, progressing into Cambridge IGCSE in Years 10-11.

Years 7-9

Depth, independence and preparation

In Secondary, students build on their earlier learning through a broad Cambridge-inspired curriculum that balances knowledge, skills and wellbeing. We encourage independence and critical thinking so students are ready for the next stage of school.

Years 10-11

The Cambridge IGCSE pathway

Students are progressively prepared for Cambridge IGCSE through focused subject teaching, assessment practice and individual support. Cambridge IGCSE is an internationally recognised two-year qualification usually taken in Years 10-11 - it builds strong subject knowledge and practical exam skills, and prepares students for further study such as Cambridge International AS & A Levels or other international pathways.

Assessment

How we assess learning

We use a mixture of formative and summative assessment to help students and teachers understand progress. Regular feedback and parent communication are central to our approach.

Cambridge assessment pathway
Greenacre prepares students progressively for the expectations of formal Cambridge assessment as they move through the secondary phase. We build skills in exam technique, extended writing and independent study.

English as an Additional Language

Greenacre is a multilingual international community. Students learning English as an additional language receive specialist support to access the curriculum and develop confidence.
